# City roadbook mode

Use for city breaks, food, architecture, museums, waterside space, and slow travel. Cluster each day by area, plan 2–4 core experiences, prefer one hotel, include a real rest window, and provide a rain alternative. State the queue and commercialization cost of popular streets instead of treating popularity as quality.

Required day fields: `items`, `rest_windows`, and `rain_alternatives`. Prioritize comfortable walking links, meal timing, luggage handling, and evening recovery.

```json
{"trip":{"primary_mode":"city"},"days":[{"rest_windows":["14:00-15:00"],"rain_alternatives":["museum"]}]}
```
