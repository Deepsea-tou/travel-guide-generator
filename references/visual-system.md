# TravelOS visual system

The memorable device is a route ribbon that turns timed stops into a horizontal journey, followed by an editorial vertical field guide. It combines outdoor-magazine tactility with an operational roadbook rather than looking like a generic card dashboard.

Themes share structure and differ through tokens: `forest` uses paper, olive, clay, and charcoal; `lake-city` uses mineral blue-green and coral; `plateau` uses sand, rust, and dark umber. Typography uses a Chinese serif/editorial stack with a compact monospace layer for time, coordinates, and labels. No remote fonts are required.

Use large, asymmetrical titles, generous negative space, thin route lines, and restrained solid panels. Avoid purple gradients, glass cards, emoji icons, excessive shadows, tiny gray text, and repeated rounded cards.

At 760px the layout becomes one column; timelines keep time, marker, and content distinct. Interactive targets are at least 44px, focus is visible, reduced motion is respected, and the page remains complete without JavaScript. Print removes controls but keeps route, schedule, safety, budget, and sources.
