# Research and freshness

Prefer official attractions, transport operators, meteorological agencies, and government notices. Then use established ticket/map services, recent first-hand travel reports, and finally clearly labeled estimates or general context. Search snippets are discovery aids, not final evidence.

Every decision-changing fact should reference a source id. Dynamic sources require a verification date unless explicitly marked as an estimate. Recheck weather and service disruptions within 72 hours of departure; schedules, opening hours, prices, tickets, and road status within 30 days. Evergreen context may be older when labeled.

If current information cannot be confirmed, state the uncertainty, keep a safe alternative, and add a pre-departure verification action. Never invent a precise train number, opening time, price, trail status, or weather forecast.
